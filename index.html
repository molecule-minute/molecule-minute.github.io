MOLECULE MINUTE

The science behind everyday things, in under a minute.

Molecule Minute is a YouTube Shorts channel with quick, animated explainers: why ice floats, why your car battery struggles in the cold, why hot chocolate changes pitch when you stir it.

Molecule Minute Autopilot

Molecule Minute Autopilot is the channel's own publishing tool. It is used only by the channel owner, to upload the channel's own videos to the Molecule Minute YouTube channel.

  • It renders each finished video and uploads it to YouTube as a scheduled Short, using the YouTube Data API.
  • It reads new video scripts from, and saves finished videos to, the channel owner's own Google Drive folders.
  • It does not access, collect or store any data about viewers or other YouTube users, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
